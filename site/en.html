Installation

BandStudio on your computer.

Choose your platform below. Analysis engines run on the computer where you prepare your songs.

Windows

Windows 11 · x64 · ZIP

Extract the whole ZIP to a writable folder. Open BandStudio.exe, then choose your engines in Réglages → Analyses locales.

WebView2 and .NET Framework 4.8 required. Python and FFmpeg included.

Download the Windows ZIP

macOS

Intel · Apple Silicon

Already installed? Open Réglages in BandStudio and check for updates. Quit and reopen the app once the download finishes.

First-time setup requires building from source with a prepared local engine. A standalone installer for a new Mac is not currently available.

Read the macOS guide

Does my audio stay on my computer?

Audio analysis runs locally. Online lyrics, chord and artwork searches use the song title and artist.

Can I edit the results?

Yes. Listen to a flagged passage, move a marker, change a chord or adjust lyric timing in the editor before sharing the sheet.

Where do REAPER and BandPilot fit?

BandStudio prepares songs and REAPER opens the exported audio project. For soundchecks and stage monitoring, see BandPilot.
