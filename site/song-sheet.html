VISUAL GUIDE · 23 NOVEMBER 2026

From lyrics and chords to a printable song sheet

Choose a chord chart, align lyrics to your recording and read the pickup into each section.

French button names match the labels in BandStudio for Windows and macOS.
01Open the song sheet 02Read an entrance before the bar 03Compare chord charts 04Look up lyrics for your recording 05Adjust a word’s timing 06Set the chords you play 07Practise with the teleprompter 08Export the PDF

STEP 01 / 08

Open the song sheet

Choose Fiche morceau (Song sheet) for the open song.

  1. Check the title, artist, key and tempo at the top of the sheet.
  2. Select a section to narrow the view. Click a bar to move the playhead there.
  3. The sheet, editor and teleprompter use the same song annotations.
IN THE APP Fiche morceau

STEP 02 / 08

Read an entrance before the bar

When the chorus starts before the next downbeat, look for the Entrée row at the start of the section.

  1. Read the bar and beat in the label. In the example, the chorus starts on beat four of bar 4.
  2. The first chord appears in this row. Lyrics you add to your own project also follow their position within the bar.
  3. Click the Entrée label to place the playhead at the entrance, then listen.
IN THE APP Fiche morceau → Entrée

STEP 03 / 08

Compare chord charts

On the sheet, Comparer les sources d’accords opens the editor’s Accords tab.

  1. Choose Rechercher une grille Internet if no sources appear. Check the title and artist.
  2. Choose Ouvrir la grille to visit the source. Listen to the same passage to identify a chart that fits your recording.
  3. Choose Utiliser cette grille to apply it. The sheet and teleprompter retain your choice.
IN THE APP Fiche morceau → Comparer les sources d’accords

STEP 04 / 08

Look up lyrics for your recording

On the sheet, choose Corriger les paroles et recaler to run the lookup again.

  1. BandStudio can strip video or chapter labels from a title and use the artist to distinguish songs with the same name.
  2. BandStudio can check several sources and compare the text against the local vocals. Timing from a matching synchronized edition helps locate repeated passages.
  3. Use Écoute ciblée to hear flagged words. To load your own timed lyrics, choose Importer des paroles and select an LRC or SRT file.
IN THE APP Fiche morceau → Corriger les paroles et recaler

STEP 05 / 08

Adjust a word’s timing

Click a line on the sheet, or select a flagged passage and choose Modifier dans l’éditeur.

  1. Listen to the excerpt and adjust its start and end. Placer au curseur uses the playhead position.
  2. Open the word timing controls to refine a single word. Moving an entire line keeps its words at their relative offsets.
  3. Save and listen again. Annuler and Rétablir undo or redo a correction.
IN THE APP Écoute ciblée → Modifier dans l’éditeur

STEP 06 / 08

Set the chords you play

Open Éditeur du morceau → Accords to set the capo and compare chord shapes.

  1. Click a chord on the sheet to edit it, or choose + Accord to add one to the bar.
  2. Check the original chord and the chord to play with your transpose or capo setting.
  3. Listen to quick changes. Manual chord edits are saved with the song.
IN THE APP Éditeur du morceau → Accords

STEP 07 / 08

Practise with the teleprompter

Open Prompteur and start playback to follow the current section, lyrics and chord.

  1. Listen for the chorus entrance as the words appear. A line without individual word timings appears as a whole at its start.
  2. To repeat a passage, open Éditeur du morceau → Sections and use its loop control.
  3. From the teleprompter, Modifier dans l’éditeur takes you back to the lyrics editor.
IN THE APP Prompteur → Modifier dans l’éditeur

STEP 08 / 08

Export the PDF

Return to Fiche morceau and choose Exporter la fiche PDF.

  1. Check the sections, pickups and chords you play. The PDF follows the saved grid and capo.
  2. Choose a filename and destination. The PDF opens in your system viewer.
  3. You can find the generated document in the song’s Exports folder. Check the rights before publicly sharing a sheet that contains protected lyrics.
IN THE APP Fiche morceau → Exporter la fiche PDF
