Make a PDF song sheet.

Choose chords, align words to bars and export a sheet for the band.

In this guide

Step 1 / 8

Open the song sheet

Choose Song sheet (Song sheet) for the open song.

  1. Check the title, artist, key and tempo at the top of the sheet.
  2. Select a section to narrow the view. Click a bar to move the playhead there.
  3. The sheet, editor and teleprompter use the same song annotations.
IN THE APP Song sheet

Step 2 / 8

Read an entrance before the bar

When the chorus starts before the next downbeat, look for the Entry row at the start of the section.

  1. Read the bar and beat in the label. In the example, the chorus starts on beat four of bar 4.
  2. The first chord appears in this row. Lyrics you add to your own project also follow their position within the bar.
  3. Click the Entry label to place the playhead at the entrance, then listen.
IN THE APP Song sheet → Entry

Step 3 / 8

Compare chord charts

On the sheet, Compare chord sources opens the editor’s Chords tab.

  1. Choose Find a chord chart online if no sources appear. Check the title and artist.
  2. Choose Open chart to visit the source. Listen to the same passage to identify a chart that fits your recording.
  3. Choose Use this chart to apply it. The sheet and teleprompter retain your choice.
IN THE APP Song sheet → Compare chord sources

Step 4 / 8

Look up lyrics for your recording

On the sheet, choose Correct and realign lyrics to run the lookup again.

  1. BandStudio can strip video or chapter labels from a title and use the artist to distinguish songs with the same name.
  2. BandStudio can check several sources and compare the text against the local vocals. Timing from a matching synchronized edition helps locate repeated passages.
  3. Use Focused listening to hear flagged words. To load your own timed lyrics, choose Import lyrics and select an LRC or SRT file.
IN THE APP Song sheet → Correct and realign lyrics

Step 5 / 8

Adjust a word’s timing

Click a line on the sheet, or select a flagged passage and choose Edit in song editor.

  1. Listen to the excerpt and adjust its start and end. Set at playhead uses the playhead position.
  2. Open the word timing controls to refine a single word. Moving an entire line keeps its words at their relative offsets.
  3. Save and listen again. Cancel and Redo undo or redo a correction.
IN THE APP Focused listening → Edit in song editor

Step 6 / 8

Set the chords you play

Open Song editor → Chords to set the capo and compare chord shapes.

  1. Click a chord on the sheet to edit it, or choose + Chord to add one to the bar.
  2. Check the original chord and the chord to play with your transpose or capo setting.
  3. Listen to quick changes. Manual chord edits are saved with the song.
IN THE APP Song editor → Chords

Step 7 / 8

Practise with the teleprompter

Open Teleprompter and start playback to follow the current section, lyrics and chord.

  1. Listen for the chorus entrance as the words appear. A line without individual word timings appears as a whole at its start.
  2. To repeat a passage, open Song editor → Sections and use its loop control.
  3. From the teleprompter, Edit in song editor takes you back to the lyrics editor.
IN THE APP Teleprompter → Edit in song editor

Step 8 / 8

Export the PDF

Return to Song sheet and choose Export PDF song sheet.

  1. Check the sections, pickups and chords you play. The PDF follows the saved grid and capo.
  2. Choose a filename and destination. The PDF opens in your system viewer.
  3. You can find the generated document in the song’s Exports folder. Check the rights before publicly sharing a sheet that contains protected lyrics.
IN THE APP Song sheet → Export PDF song sheet
