VISUAL GUIDE · 21 NOVEMBER 2026

Your sheet, ready to play.

Lyrics, chords and timing, through to PDF.

macOS and Windows app · follow at your own pace. French button labels match the app.

STEP 01 / 08

Open the song sheet

The sheet brings the song together bar by bar.

  1. After Tout préparer, open Fiche morceau.
  2. Check title, artist, tempo and key. Available artwork is displayed.
  3. Annotations belong to the open project; your edits are saved with it.
IN THE APP Fiche morceau

STEP 02 / 08

Find a passage

Move between sections without losing your place.

  1. Choose a section from the list.
  2. Click a bar or section title to move the listening cursor.
  3. Listen before changing text or timing.
IN THE APP Choose a passage

STEP 03 / 08

Compare chord charts

Online charts may describe different arrangements.

  1. Check the sources in Paroles & accords.
  2. Ouvrir la grille opens the source; Utiliser cette grille keeps your choice for this song.
  3. Audio agreement helps automatic selection. Uncertain chords still need listening.
IN THE APP Paroles & accords

STEP 04 / 08

Correct the lyrics

Lookup and alignment may already be complete.

  1. Tout préparer uses compatible references when available.
  2. Corriger avec Internet et recaler retries lookup/alignment from the lyric tools.
  3. Click a sheet line to edit its text. Manual lines are protected.
IN THE APP Corriger avec Internet et recaler

STEP 05 / 08

Adjust timing by hand

Move an entrance if it still needs adjustment.

  1. Use start/end bars and beats, nudge controls or Placer au curseur.
  2. Moving a line preserves its synchronized words’ relative timing.
  3. Manual timing is saved. Annuler and Rétablir remain available.
IN THE APP Recaler les paroles à la main

STEP 06 / 08

Refine the chords

Keep the interpretation you need.

  1. Click a chord to edit its text and timing.
  2. + Accord adds a chord. Portions outside a replaced interval are preserved.
  3. Listen to inversions and quick changes; manual corrections take priority.
IN THE APP + Accord

STEP 07 / 08

Prepare your reading view

Keep the information useful for practice.

  1. Add the lyrics or notes you need. Check sections and cues.
  2. Displayed chords follow the selected pitch shift and capo.
  3. A line without word timing remains whole at its starting position.
IN THE APP Listen and review

STEP 08 / 08

Save the PDF

The printable sheet follows your annotations.

  1. Click Exporter la fiche PDF, then choose a filename and folder.
  2. The PDF opens in your system viewer. Afficher l’export helps you locate it.
  3. Cancelling the destination dialog keeps the generated PDF in the song’s Exports folder.
IN THE APP Exporter la fiche PDF

Optional models must be installed. Uncertain passages remain flagged; universal musical accuracy is not guaranteed.
