BandStudio 1.1.0 — prepare a song for rehearsal and REAPER

GET STARTED
On Windows, extract the entire ZIP into a writable folder and open BandStudio.exe.
Windows requires WebView2 and .NET Framework 4.8. In Settings → Local analysis,
install the engines you need. The first download requires an internet connection.
On an existing Mac installation, open Settings and choose Check now. Quit and reopen
BandStudio after the update downloads. For a first-time Mac build, see the setup
guide at https://github.com/herve8940/BandStudio/blob/main/docs/BANDSTUDIO.md#macos.

Choose English or Français in Settings → Interface language, then restart BandStudio.
Song titles, lyrics, notes and custom markers keep the text you entered. Set the
spoken announcement language separately in Song editor → Metronome.

PREPARE YOUR FIRST SONG
1. Open My songs and choose Import a song. Leave automatic preparation enabled
   to start as soon as the audio has been imported. For several files, open
   Prepare a batch, choose your settings, add files and select Start queue.
2. In Song editor, solo or mute tracks and loop the passage you want to practise.
   Listen for the downbeat and correct the tempo, sections, lyrics or cues.
   Protect a section or its timing to keep your edits during a new analysis.
3. In Metronome, set the click, count-in and announcements, then choose Apply to song.
   The detected grid follows the recording. Choose a fixed tempo for a steady track.
4. Open Song sheet to edit a chord or a lyric and export a printable PDF.
5. Open REAPER project and choose Check and export project or Check and open in REAPER.
   Each export gets its own folder. Keep the .rpp file with the Audio folder.

LISTEN AND EXPORT
Space starts or pauses playback. M adds a marker. Use Alt + Left/Right to move
between markers. Try Without my instrument or My instrument only in the editor.
Speed and transposition affect playback and the REAPER export. Individual stems
keep their original tempo and pitch. Model quality varies with the recording.

Under Audio & documents you can export stems, LRC/SRT lyrics, drum MIDI or a
project ZIP. Audio processing is local. Online searches for lyrics, charts and
cover art use the song title and artist. Check proposed results by ear.

ABLETON LIVE AND SHOW EXPORTS
Under Export, choose the current song, a library selection or a setlist. Reorder the
songs and save the list for the next show. Ableton Live Project creates one .als
Arrangement Set per song. Keep the Set with its Samples folder to access tracks,
guides, locators and tempo changes.
QLab + grandMA3 Show creates one QLab group and one lighting sequence per song.
Choose a stereo mix, stems or both, and full-show timecode, per-song timecode or both.
Use a timed word or chord as the starting point for a video or lighting marker.
On the show Mac, open an empty workspace in QLab 5 and run Build QLab.command.
The exported README.txt covers FOH, monitor and LTC routing and importing the sequences
and timecode shows on the console. Check the outputs in rehearsal.
Each export creates a new folder and preserves previous exports and edits.
